Hoopline GM — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026 · Controller: Víctor López Rodríguez · Contact: hooplinegm@gmail.com

Hoopline GM is a basketball manager game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else sees it, and how you get rid of it. It covers the app only — not any website or service it links to.

2. Who processes it

The app is run by one person, not a company with departments. These are the services that hold or see data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication and server functions. This is where your account and saved game live.
  • RevenueCat — handles subscription and purchase state across both stores.
  • Google — Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications; Google AdMob serves the rewarded ads; Google Play processes Android purchases.
  • Apple — processes iOS purchases and delivers push notifications on iOS.

Some of these process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the services above.

3. Advertising and your consent

The app shows rewarded ads only — an ad you choose to watch in exchange for energy, gems, or a doubled reward. There are no banners, and no ad you cannot decline. Nothing about the game is locked behind watching one.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form before requesting any ad, and passes that choice to Google. Choosing not to consent means you see non-personalised ads; it does not remove the feature. You can reopen that form at any time from the app's settings.

4. How long we keep it

Your account and game data are kept for as long as the account exists. Delete the account and they are deleted with it. Purchase and tax records are kept for as long as tax law requires, which in Spain is currently four years, and are kept separately from your game data. Ad reward records are kept for 30 days, which is what the daily limit needs, and then removed.

5. Your rights, and how to use them

Under the GDPR you may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may also withdraw a consent at any time, which does not affect what was done before you did.

Deleting your Hoopline GM account

How to ask, in the app. Open More, scroll to Account, and tap Delete account. Confirming deletes everything immediately — there is nothing to fill in, no reason to give, and no waiting period, because the app calls the same function that removes the account rather than a person actioning a request by hand.

How to ask without the app. If you cannot or would rather not sign in, email hooplinegm@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with the words delete my account in the subject. We action it within 30 days and reply to confirm when it is done. Deleting the app from your phone does neither — it does not delete the account either way; the saved game lives on our servers so you can come back to it on another device.

What is deleted. Everything the account is: your sign-in and password, your username and favourite team, every league you created and every team, squad, fixture, result, transfer and bid inside it, your achievements and trophies, your balances of coins, gems and energy, the cosmetics you own and have equipped, your push notification token, and the record of which rewarded ads you watched. All of it goes at once, permanently, and none of it can be recovered afterwards.

What is kept, and for how long. One thing: the record of purchases you made — which product, when, and the transaction identifier Apple or Google gave it. Spanish tax law requires a seller to keep proof of a sale, currently for four years, and that obligation does not end because an account does. Those records carry no game data and no contact details, and once the account is deleted the identifier in them refers to nothing that still exists. They are erased when the retention period ends.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

6. Children

Hoopline GM is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Storage on your device

The app stores your session token and your settings on the device itself so that you stay signed in. This is not a cookie and is not readable by anyone else. Google's ad SDK stores its own identifiers on the device, governed by the choice you make in the consent form.

8. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top changes and the app tells you the next time you open it. The current version always lives at this address.
