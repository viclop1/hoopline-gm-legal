Hoopline GM — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Controller: Víctor López Rodríguez - Madrid, Spain · Contact: hooplinegm@gmail.com

Hoopline GM is a basketball manager game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else sees it, and how you get rid of it. It covers the app and, in section 8, the website hooplinegm.com — not any other website or service it links to.

2. Who processes it

The app is run by one person, not a company with departments. These are the services that hold or see data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication and server functions. This is where your account and saved game live.
  • RevenueCat — handles subscription and purchase state across both stores.
  • Google — Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications; Google AdMob serves the rewarded ads; Google Play processes Android purchases.
  • Apple — processes iOS purchases and delivers push notifications on iOS. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account on an iPhone or iPad also asks Apple to revoke this app's access to your Apple ID.
  • Resend — delivers the purchase confirmation emails.
  • PostHog — receives the product analytics and crash reports described above. We use its European Union hosting, so that data is processed inside the EEA and does not leave it.

Some of these process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

We do not sell your data. We do not disclose it to third parties other than the services above, except as this policy describes: in particular, what other players see of you when you use the features that show it (your team's name and the other information described in section 1 for duels, rivalries, the weekly challenge's table and Friends), and any card you choose to share.

3. Advertising and your consent

The app shows rewarded ads only — an ad you choose to watch in exchange for energy, gems, or a doubled reward. There are no banners, and no ad you cannot decline. Nothing about the game is locked behind watching one.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form before requesting any ad, and passes that choice to Google. Choosing not to consent means you see non-personalised ads; it does not remove the feature. You can reopen that form at any time from the app's settings, while ads are enabled for your account.

If you are 16 or 17, the app indicates to Google's ad service that you are under 18 and requests non-personalised ads. All ads, whatever your age, are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

If you are 16 or 17 and object to this use of your age (see section 5), we stop it by turning ads off for your account: from then on, no ad is offered or requested on any device where you sign in to it, and you no longer receive ad rewards. Nothing else in the game changes. The ads stay off after you turn 18, until you ask us to turn them back on. We still keep your age record, because the minimum age and Friends need it (section 1).

Two limits to this, until the app knows it is your account. On a new device, before you sign in, the app does not yet know who you are, so Google's consent form may open there once and its ad service may start, both as for someone under 18; your age is not used, and no ad is requested. Once you sign in, ads are off on that device too, and neither starts again. A version of the app from before this change, on a device you have not updated, may still start Google's consent form and ad service when it opens, although our servers no longer offer or pay any ad to your account; this stops once it is updated.

4. How long we keep it

Your account and game data, including your age answer and the record of the terms you accepted, are kept for as long as the account exists. Delete the account and they are deleted with it.

Duels and reports: Duel records are kept for 12 months from the day the duel was played and are then deleted. Duel results may remain in other players' match history after an account is deleted. In that case, the deleted account's team name is replaced with "Deleted team", and only the match result and date are retained in that history. We retain reports for up to 12 months, unless a longer period is necessary to handle an ongoing incident or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Where an account is deleted, we remove or anonymise the reporting account's identifier when it is no longer needed, while retaining only the information necessary to handle the report and any related moderation action.

Weekly challenge: the details of your attempts (the players you picked, your tactic, the games and the team name shown in the table) are kept for 30 days after the week closes, in case a technical incident or a claim needs them, and are then deleted automatically, unless a specific incident requires keeping some of them for longer; in that case we keep only what that incident needs, and only until it is resolved. The record of the extra attempt obtained with a rewarded ad goes at the same time. Your weekly results (place, prize tier, coins and Legacy points) are kept for as long as the account exists, because they count towards your Legacy. The weekly table shows only the week in progress.

Friends and invitations: a friend request lapses after 30 days if it is not answered. A friendship lasts until either of you removes it, blocks the other or deletes their account; removing a friend removes the friendship for both, without telling the other. Wall events are kept for 12 months and then deleted, and reactions go with their event. Friendlies are kept for 12 months. The counters that limit attempts are kept for two days. The record of an invitation is deleted 12 months after the shirt was given (or 12 months after it was made, if no shirt was ever given), unless it is needed to handle a claim; after that, only the fact that your account received the shirt remains, with the shirt itself. A friendship that cannot be seen for the time being (because one of you paused Friends, or because one of you turned 18 while the other is still 16 or 17) is kept, hidden from both, until it can be seen again, until one of you removes it after it becomes visible again or deletes their account, or until either of you asks us to remove it (write to us and we will).

Purchase records, purchase consents and purchase confirmation emails are the exception. They are kept for five years from the purchase, whether or not the account still exists, because they are the evidence of a contract: five years is the general period in which a claim about it can be brought in Spain (Civil Code, art. 1964), and it also covers the four years tax law requires for a record of a sale. Once the account is deleted they are blocked: kept apart from everything else, not used for anything, and available only to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to courts and public authorities that are entitled to them (Spanish Data Protection Act, LOPDGDD, art. 32). After five years they are deleted automatically, unless a claim or a legal obligation still requires them.

Ad reward records are kept for 30 days, which is what the daily limit needs, and then removed. Product analytics and crash reports are kept for up to 12 months and are then deleted. Two different things stop or remove them: switching analytics off in Settings stops new ones being recorded, and leaves the ones already recorded to be deleted when their 12 months are up; deleting your account deletes the account and its data, stops anything new, and lets you ask us to delete the analytics and crash reports already recorded for it (see section 5).

5. Your rights, and how to use them

Under the GDPR you may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may also withdraw a consent at any time, which does not affect what was done before you did.

Deleting your Hoopline GM account

How to ask, in the app. Open More, scroll to Account, and tap Delete account. Confirming deletes everything immediately — there is nothing to fill in, no reason to give, and no waiting period, because the app calls the same function that removes the account rather than a person actioning a request by hand.

How to ask without the app. If you cannot or would rather not sign in, email hooplinegm@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with the words delete my account in the subject. We action it within 30 days and reply to confirm when it is done. Deleting the app from your phone does neither — it does not delete the account either way; the saved game lives on our servers so you can come back to it on another device.

What is deleted. All the ordinary data of the account, at once and permanently:

  • your sign-in and password, your username and favourite team, your age answer and the record of the terms you accepted;
  • every league you created, and every team, squad, fixture, result, transfer and bid inside it; your achievements, first steps and trophies; your balances of coins, gems and energy; the cosmetics you own and have equipped;
  • your push notification token and the record of which rewarded ads you watched;
  • the managers you blocked, your duels and your weekly challenge attempts and results;
  • your manager code, your friend requests and friendships (you disappear from your friends' lists), your wall and the reactions you gave, your friendlies and your invitations.

None of it can be recovered afterwards. Ask us at the same time and we will also delete the analytics and crash reports tied to your account identifier, which are held by a separate service and do not go automatically.

What is kept, and for how long. Only what is still needed as evidence or to protect other users, kept apart and used for nothing else:

  • Evidence of your purchases: which product you bought, when, at what price and with which transaction identifier; the consent you gave before paying; and the confirmation email we sent you. They are kept blocked for five years from each purchase, as section 4 explains, carry no game data, and are then erased.
  • Duels in other players' history: a duel you played stays in your rival's history, with its result and date and with "Deleted team" in place of your team's name, until its 12 months are up.
  • Reports: a report you made, or one made about your team, is kept for as long as section 4 says, without your account identifier, with only what is needed to handle it.

Nothing else is kept. In particular, the record of the terms you accepted is deleted with the account.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

6. Minimum age: 16

Hoopline GM is for people aged 16 or over. When you create an account, the app asks for the month and year you were born; if you are under 16 you cannot continue. We do not keep that date, only whether you are under 18 and, if so, until when. That answer is your declaration; we do not verify your identity or age. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Storage on your device

The app stores your session token and your settings on the device itself so that you stay signed in. It also remembers on the device whether ads must be requested as for someone under 18 (only yes or no, never your age; yes if you are 16 or 17), so that Google's consent form, which opens before you sign in, is asked in the same way. If ads are off for your account (section 3), it keeps only that instead, and does not start Google's ad service or its consent form (except in the two cases described at the end of section 3: a new device before you sign in, and an older version of the app). It also keeps there how much the first steps should guide you (your answer when you named your team), which of the cards shown the first time you open a screen you have already seen, and whether you have answered or closed How did you hear about Hoopline GM?; these stay on the device and are not sent to us. To share a card, it writes the image to a temporary file that your phone's share menu reads; nothing in it is sent to us. This is not a cookie and is not readable by anyone else. Google's ad SDK stores its own identifiers on the device, governed by the choice you make in the consent form.

8. Website (hooplinegm.com)

This section covers the website hooplinegm.com, not the game. The website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and its fonts and images are served from the same domain.

When you visit the website, technical data about the connection is processed to deliver the pages and to keep the service secure: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and technical details such as the browser and device type your browser sends. The website is delivered through Cloudflare, Inc., which provides our infrastructure and security services. Cloudflare may process technical information about visitors, including IP addresses and security-related event data, as necessary to provide and secure the service. Depending on the processing involved, Cloudflare may act on our behalf or as an independent controller for its own security and operational purposes. Further information is available in Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

We use this data only to deliver the content, protect the website against abuse and keep it working. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running and securing the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We have not enabled HTTP request log retention, Cloudflare Web Analytics or Logpush for this website. We therefore do not maintain a separate log of visitors or send website request logs to another service. Cloudflare may nevertheless process technical and security-related data as part of operating and protecting its network. Security Events available through Cloudflare may be retained for up to 31 days. For further information, please see Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

The page https://hooplinegm.com/auth/confirm opens when you tap the link in a confirmation or password-recovery email. It sends the one-time code in the link to Supabase, our authentication provider, to confirm your account or to let you set a new password, and removes the code from the address bar. It does not keep any of this information itself and does not use cookies.
